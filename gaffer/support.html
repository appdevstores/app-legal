Common questions

Is my data in the cloud?

No. Everything — seasons, rosters, matches, and reports — is stored only on your device. There are no accounts and no servers.

What does Gaffer Pro include?

Gaffer Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks additional seasons and teams. There is no subscription.

How do I restore Gaffer Pro?

Open the Gaffer Pro upgrade prompt and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple or Google account that made the purchase.

How do I clear old matches?

Open a season, go to Storage Management, select matches, and delete them. You can also delete an entire season from the same screen.

Can I email a match report?

Yes. At full time, tap Email Image Summary to share a polished match summary from your device's mail app or share sheet.

Is Sync available?

Not yet. Gaffer is currently local-only. Cloud Sync is planned for a future version and is not currently available.
