Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

GafferLab is a soccer session-planning app designed for coaches. This policy describes the current TestFlight version, 1.0.0.

Information stored on your device

Saved session names, selected drills, and drill durations are stored in the app’s local database on your device. You can delete saved sessions from the Saved screen. Device backups, if enabled, are governed by your device and Apple account settings.

Information sent to App Dev Stores

The current version does not require an account and does not send session content to an App Dev Stores server. It does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

PDF export and sharing

Session PDFs are generated on your device. If you choose to share a PDF, iOS sends it to the app or destination you select. The privacy practices of that destination then apply.

Children’s information

GafferLab is a tool for adult coaches, not a social or account-based app for children. It does not request player profiles or children’s contact information. Coaches should avoid entering sensitive information about players in session names or other content.

Purchases and future services

In-app purchase checkout, analytics, user accounts, and GafferLab Sync are not active in this TestFlight version. This policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before any of those services are enabled to explain the data involved and any third-party providers.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact App Dev Stores at appdevstores@gmail.com.
