About this TestFlight build

This build does not require an account. Sessions are stored locally on your device. Pro checkout and GafferLab Sync are not available in this beta.

For billing or refund support for purchases made through Apple, use Apple’s purchase-support tools. GafferLab Pro purchases are not enabled in this TestFlight build.
